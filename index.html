The problem

Design happens in the studio. The work happens outside it.

The work happens with fabricators, masons, artisans, vendors and suppliers. Each party speaks a different language.

Studios

  • Design

Makers

  • Clarity
  • Coordination
  • Communication
  • Money

Clients

  • Design
  • Money
  • Protecting their interests

The studio translates between all three by hand, carrying the risk and the expectations that never show up in a render. Meaning is lost between the drawing and the site, and that's where money and trust leak.

What Analogi does

One design intent, translated for each stakeholder.

Analogi gives for makers

Clear scopes and quantities, not a drawing set to interpret

Analogi gives for clients

Cost and progress they can verify and sign off

Analogi gives for the studio

A locked record of what was agreed

Grounded in real project data. If it can't verify something, it says so instead of guessing.

How it works

Drawings go in. A costed, locked BOQ comes out.

01.

Drawings in

The studio uploads its drawings.

02.

Checked

Analogi checks them for completeness and asks for what's missing.

03.

Costed

It produces a costed BOQ from the studio's own rate card.

04.

Frozen

Once approved, it's frozen. Any edit reopens the sign-off automatically.
